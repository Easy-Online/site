Conversion notes

  • Embedded text: fastest and usually most accurate.
  • Scanned PDFs: OCR accuracy depends on scan clarity, skew, contrast and device performance.
  • Tables: spatial heuristics work best on regular invoices, statements, schedules and reports.
  • Large files: OCR is CPU- and memory-intensive; split very large documents when practical.
  • Privacy: this static implementation does not upload source PDFs to EasyFile storage.
