    What this module produces

    DateDescriptionAmount
    03/07/2026CLIENT PAYMENT 45814500.00
    04/07/2026MONTHLY SERVICE FEE-175.00
    • Headers are spelled Date, Description and Amount.
    • Descriptions are one line, trimmed and limited to 100 characters.
    • Amounts use a decimal point with no currency symbol or thousand separator.
    • Zero-value rows and exact duplicates are blocked or flagged.
