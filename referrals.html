A referral counts only when a different verified user enters through your link and the referral service accepts one qualifying action such as Save Draft, Preview, Print or Export.

How the referral scheme works

1

Use one module

Choose any EasyFile module and complete one meaningful document action. This consumes the once-off free use.

2

Refer three people

Share your unique link with three different verified people. Self-referrals and repeat use by the same referral do not count twice.

3

Unlock continued access

When all three referrals qualify, the server-authoritative referral service unlocks continued access to the full EasyFile module suite.

Secure access verification

Referral validation requires an internet connection. EasyFile now fails closed when access cannot be verified. Offline unlocked access remains disabled unless the referral service issues a signed, unexpired entitlement token.
