# EasyFile customer discovery

## Research objective

Determine whether bank-statement-to-business-records is a frequent, painful workflow for South African small businesses and bookkeepers, and which completed outcome they will pay for.

## Recruit

Interview at least 15 participants:

- five owner-managed service businesses
- five independent bookkeepers
- three accounting practices serving small businesses
- two businesses that already use Sage or a competing accounting product

Do not recruit only current EasyFile users.

## Interview prompts

1. Walk me through the last time you turned bank activity into accounting records.
2. What files and systems were involved?
3. Which part required the most manual checking?
4. What errors occur and what do they cost?
5. Who reviews or approves the result?
6. How long does the workflow take per account and month?
7. Which information would you never upload to a new service?
8. What have you already tried?
9. What would make you switch?
10. What would a successful first ten minutes look like?

Avoid pitching EasyFile until the participant has described the current workflow.

## Evidence to capture

- workflow frequency
- minutes per statement
- transaction volume
- correction rate
- current tools and export formats
- decision maker and buyer
- security objections
- willingness to trial
- willingness to pay

## Decision rule

Proceed with the wedge when at least 10 participants report the workflow monthly, at least seven accept a supervised product trial, and at least five agree to a paid pilot or provide a credible procurement path.
