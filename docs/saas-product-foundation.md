# EasyFile SaaS product foundation

## Product promise

EasyFile turns bank activity into reviewed, business-ready records for South African small businesses and bookkeepers.

## Initial customer and activation

The first target customer is an owner-managed service business or bookkeeper who repeatedly converts bank activity into accounting and operational records.

Activation occurs when a new user imports a statement, reviews the extracted transactions, resolves all blocking warnings and downloads a valid Sage-ready CSV or Excel file in less than 10 minutes.

## Product principles

1. Start with the working surface, not a marketing tour.
2. Ask for an account only when cloud persistence or collaboration creates clear value.
3. Keep local processing explicit and never imply local records are backed up.
4. Require human review for inferred financial data.
5. Make every important record exportable.
6. Treat referrals as rewards, never as a barrier to essential use.

## Planned plans

Pricing must be validated before exact prices are published.

- Free: local conversion and a limited monthly cloud allowance.
- Solo: one organisation, durable records, history and exports.
- Business: multiple members, roles, approvals and shared records.
- Accountant: multiple client organisations, bulk processing and consolidated administration.

## Release gates

- No cross-organisation data access is possible.
- Account export and deletion work without support intervention.
- Backup restoration is tested.
- Critical workflows have automated acceptance coverage.
- Security and privacy claims match actual behaviour.
