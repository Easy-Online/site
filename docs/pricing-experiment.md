# EasyFile pricing experiment

Do not publish fixed prices before customer evidence exists.

## Value metric

Test monthly organisations, statement conversions and accountant client workspaces. Avoid charging per button, export format or stored contact.

## Interview cards

Show each participant the same plan boundaries in a random order:

- Free: local converter and sample workflows.
- Solo: one cloud workspace, durable history and standard exports.
- Business: team roles, approvals and shared records.
- Accountant: multiple client workspaces and bulk operations.

Ask which plan fits, which capability changes the answer and what existing cost or labour it replaces.

## Pilot offer

Offer a time-limited paid pilot only after the participant completes the activation event. Record the offered price, accepted price, objections, customer type and expected monthly volume.

## Decision rule

Choose initial prices only after at least 10 qualified price conversations and three paid commitments. Prefer a simple monthly price with transparent usage boundaries and no surprise overages.
