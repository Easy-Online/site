# EasyFile product metrics

## Activation funnel

Track converter_viewed, sample_loaded or statement_selected, conversion_started, conversion_completed, review_warning_resolved, export_completed, workspace_created and subscription_started.

Never include statement text, account numbers, transaction descriptions or exported data in analytics.

## Primary measures

- Activation rate: qualified visitors who complete a valid export.
- Time to value: converter_viewed to first export_completed.
- Import success rate: completed conversions divided by attempts.
- Review burden: warnings per imported transaction.
- Day-7 and day-30 retained organisations.
- Free-to-paid conversion, recurring revenue and revenue retention.
- Support requests per 100 active organisations.

## Initial targets

- Median time to first export below 10 minutes.
- At least 60% activation among users who select a valid statement.
- Import technical failure below 2%.
- No unresolved critical accessibility or security defects at release.

Analytics consent and retention must be defined before events are sent externally.
